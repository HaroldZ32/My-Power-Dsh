## 3. Classification

Rules that decide membership (they come from the artifacts, not from taste):

* **(a) mpd-branded** — the token is a **rename source** in `MPD_SCRUB` (`build-mcp.mjs:104-136`) or a
  **residual assertion** there: the build MUST rename it (or prove its absence after renaming).
* **(b) deliberate foreign contract** — the token must stay **byte-identical**; renaming it breaks a
  contract with another product (codex, the LSP daemon wire, upstream package layout, upstream identifiers).
* **(c) defect** — the build path would emit or leave a foreign spelling; the exact correct form and the
  HEAD carrier of the defect are named in §4.
* **Granularity (evidentiary rule).** The instrument is the scrub's **key list**, not a regex: spellings like
  `@oh-my-opencode` carry the upstream brand yet contain no `omo` trigram, so a regex misses them — and a
  broad rewrite is exactly what corrupts an English word (`platformFromOptions` → `platformFrmpdOptions`,
  row C-2). Any automated brand check therefore has to be **list- and diff-based**, never a bare shape scan.

## 5. ONE-WRITER rule and branch ownership
