## Units and coverage

- **Tool surface** measures the UTF-8 bytes of ECMAScript `JSON.stringify(data.header.tools)` with no replacer or indentation. Family sums count compact standalone tool objects. Array overhead is two brackets and one comma per adjacent pair. The original November array adds 126 bytes and the October array adds 118. At four bytes per token the figures are schema-only estimates. Provider framing, instructions, and actual token counts are outside this measurement. Invalid input or an unrepresentable estimate yields `UNMEASURED` on the affected side. [Family totals and input digests](../baseline/T0.2/20261123T054215Z/surface.json)
- **Dispatch and attribution** count retained passes and recorded pairings per pass. A pass needs both contemporaneous sets to compute its ready-but-unpaired difference. Missing skip reasons remain visible as unknown. The current board or dispatch ledger does not establish an earlier pass's ready set. Repeating a journal through the optional capture argument contributes one source. Separate files retain their own source coordinates. [Dispatch controls](../baseline/wave0-metrics/replay.md)
- **Ceremony** separates board records from archived plan approvals, and unapproved shells from the structurally empty subset. Timings use `approvedAt − createdAt` divided by 60,000, in minutes. Corrupt or missing expected records leave an unknown total and retain a confirmed lower bound. Explicit planning and minting can confirm a cell link. A stale pointer, unverified fallback, or ambiguous link requires separate evidence. [Ceremony and provenance controls](../baseline/wave0-metrics/independent-verification.md)
- **Growth** divides `usedTokens` by the recorded `turn` and divides `usedTokens` by `windowTokens` for the ratio. The rate describes accumulation up to a checkpoint. A zero turn counts as a checkpoint and is skipped for arithmetic. Missing operands affect the relevant distributions. Missing, unreadable, or malformed discovered journals leave checkpoint totals and distributions incomplete while preserving valid rows and their statistics. The dated event inputs do not establish an effective host threshold. [Growth coverage controls](../baseline/wave0-metrics/coverage-replay.ts)
- **Worker and reseed evidence** distinguishes board claims, retained barrier outcomes, session receipts, and actual host spawn calls. Reassign counts are Cellwork call summaries. The 19 open assignments and all their unknown progress signals describe the two dated collector scans. The receipt controls prove the correlation rules with invented inputs.
