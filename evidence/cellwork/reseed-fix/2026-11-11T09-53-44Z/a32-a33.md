## A33 — the kept child is repairable when BOTH current records name the old handle (Major-2)

Seed: the A29 shape — a reseed whose rollback is REFUSED over a concurrent writer's document that names
`OLD_HANDLE`; both TEAM row and CELL row end at `OLD_HANDLE`; the barrier journals
`checkpoint-unrecoverable` with `orphanChild=NEW_HANDLE` + `reconcileRequired:true`; the child is kept
and the registry holds it live.

Assertions:
1. Premise: TEAM `executorRef === OLD_HANDLE`, CELL `sessionId === OLD_HANDLE`.
2. `cellwork_dispatch {rebind:[{member, handle:NEW_HANDLE}], unfence:[member]}` answers
   `rebound:true`, `handleOrphanAdopted:true`, `namedBy:["journal:checkpoint-unrecoverable"]`.
3. TEAM row now `NEW_HANDLE` / `mode:"member"`; CELL mirror `NEW_HANDLE` and unfenced.
4. Board: T1 is `in_progress` with owner `OLD_HANDLE`, so it is reported in `tasksNeedingRelease`
   and NOT moved (the Major-1 rule on the same write).
5. Evidence is NOT loosened: a FOREIGN UUID is still refused `handle-unevidenced` on the same record,
   and the repaired TEAM row is untouched by the refusal.
6. A21 / A26 remain green in the same suite run (47/47 arms pass).
